# Customer Service Playbook — Portfolio Demo

This is a demonstration playbook for the portfolio project. It is not an
employer's official operating procedure.

## Empathy

Acknowledge the customer's impact in one natural sentence before moving to the
solution. Avoid repeating apologies when the customer needs a concrete action.
An empathy flag is coaching evidence, not a pass/fail QA item.

## Probing

Ask the smallest number of precise questions needed to identify the problem.
Explain why a sensitive or unexpected question is necessary. Do not repeat a
question when the customer already supplied the answer.

## Ownership

State what you will do next, what the customer must do, and what remains under
your ownership. Do not promise an outcome or timeline that is outside the
agent's control.

## Next steps

Close with a concise owner-action-timing recap. Confirm that the customer
understands the next step and knows how to reconnect if the expected result
does not occur.

## Efficient resolution

Efficiency comes from structure, not rushing. Use one issue recap, one focused
diagnostic sequence, and one closing recap. Never reduce handle time by
skipping required troubleshooting, verification, documentation, empathy, or
compliance steps.
