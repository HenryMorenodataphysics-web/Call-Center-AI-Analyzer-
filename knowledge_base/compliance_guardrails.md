# Compliance Guardrails — Portfolio Demo

These are conservative demonstration guardrails and do not replace legal,
privacy, security, or employer-approved procedures.

## Privacy

Do not expose customer personal data, authentication answers, payment details,
or raw transcripts in general coaching responses. Use call IDs and turn
references as evidence pointers; authorized reviewers can inspect source data
through the governed workflow.

## Unsupported requests

If an official KPI is unavailable, state that it is unavailable. Do not infer
it from sentiment, tone, risk, or a synthetic fixture. If policy is missing or
ambiguous, recommend escalation to the responsible supervisor or policy owner.

## Safe efficiency

Never recommend reducing customer service, required verification, compliance,
or necessary troubleshooting to improve AHT. Efficiency advice must preserve
quality and customer-protection guardrails.
