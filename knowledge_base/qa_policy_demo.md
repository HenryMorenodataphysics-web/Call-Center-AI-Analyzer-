# QA Policy — Portfolio Demo

This policy exists only to demonstrate retrieval and guardrails. Production use
requires the employer's approved QA scorecard and policy owners.

## Required quality behaviors

Verify the customer using only the approved process. Accurately explain the
resolution, document material actions, and confirm next steps. Avoid guarantees
that are not supported by policy or system state.

## KPI interpretation

CSAT, NPS, QA, and Five Stars must come from governed business systems. A model
proxy, sentiment label, acoustic signal, or synthetic demo value must never be
presented as an official outcome.

## Coaching use

Use analyzed calls to find examples and practice opportunities. A missing
rule-based behavior flag does not prove that the behavior was absent, and a
single call should not be used for disciplinary conclusions.
