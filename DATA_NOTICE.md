# Data notice

The MIT License in `LICENSE` applies to the source code, not to third-party
datasets or their derivatives.

The analytical sample in this repository is derived from AppTek's role-played
[Call Center Dialogues dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/apptek-com/apptek_callcenter_dialogues).
The upstream dataset card identifies the data as CC BY-SA 4.0 and describes its
intended use and limitations. Retain the original attribution and source IDs,
review the upstream terms, and do not assume the code license covers the data.

Original audio is excluded from Git. Checked-in CSV, JSON, and HTML artifacts
support portfolio review and reproducibility. Synthetic surveys and synthetic
business KPIs are labeled as fixtures and are not real customer outcomes.

The sample contains role-played conversations rather than real customer calls.
Do not replace it with confidential or personal data without appropriate
authorization, access controls, retention rules, and privacy review.
